Television in a building depends on three things: the aerial, the equipment that distributes the signal and the cable running to each home. If one of them fails, every neighbour sees the same thing: a blocky picture, dropouts or a black screen.

We do not guess. We measure the signal before touching anything, so we know whether the problem is the aerial, the equipment or the cable. And when we finish, you get those readings in writing.
