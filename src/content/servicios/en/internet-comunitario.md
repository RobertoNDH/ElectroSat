The first objection at any owners meeting is the building work: trunking up the stairwell, holes in the walls and weeks of disruption. That does not happen here, because the building already has a route for the cabling.

On the visit we check the condition of the installation and where it runs from. With that we prepare a report written to be read at a meeting, not only by a technician.
