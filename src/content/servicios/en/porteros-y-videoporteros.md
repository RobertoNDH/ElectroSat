When a building door entry system fails there are two routes: track down spares for equipment nobody makes any more, or replace the system. We give you both with prices, because sometimes a repair buys another two years and sometimes it is money thrown away.

If it is replaced, setting up every resident and engraving the panels is included. That is what generates most of the follow-up calls, and we close it before we leave.
