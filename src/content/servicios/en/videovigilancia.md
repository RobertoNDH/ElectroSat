In a residential building, cameras are approved at the owners meeting and fitted once. It is worth getting it right first time: thinking through where each one goes, hiding the cabling and configuring it so it still helps if you ever need to make a claim.

Before quoting we walk the building and mark where a camera actually earns its place. Usually it comes to fewer than you expected, but better positioned.
